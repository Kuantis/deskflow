1. Who we are

In short: DeskFlow is made by Kuantis. For any privacy question, write to kuantis.dev@gmail.com.

This policy explains how the DeskFlow app for Android (package com.deskflow.app) and this website handle personal data.

  • Developer and controller: Kuantis (“we”, “us”), based in Colombia. Under laws such as the GDPR, we are the controller for the processing we decide on, which this policy describes.
  • Contact: kuantis.dev@gmail.com
  • EU and UK representatives (Art. 27 GDPR and UK GDPR): none appointed. We run no servers, and our processing of personal data is limited and occasional (Art. 27(2)), so users in the EU and the UK can contact us directly at kuantis.dev@gmail.com.

DeskFlow is made by a small team, so the same contact handles every privacy matter, including as data protection contact (encarregado, Brazil), person in charge of the protection of personal information (Quebec), privacy officer (South Korea), Information Officer (South Africa) and grievance officer (India).

Google (AdMob, Google Play and Android backup) and RevenueCat are separate companies with their own privacy policies. Section 4 explains what each one does.

The app also includes a privacy notice: Settings → About → Privacy policy, or the privacy link on the Watch & health screen. The same screen opens when you tap the privacy link on Health Connect's permission screen. This web page is the complete version.

3. Data stored on your phone

In short: Everything you set up and log in DeskFlow is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. We don't have servers and never receive it.

DeskFlow works without an account or sign-up, and we don't run servers. What you enter is stored only on your device, in DeskFlow's private storage, an area of your phone that other apps can't read. This includes:

  • your work schedules and shifts, lunch time and days off;
  • your goals and reminder settings (water, coffee or tea, active breaks, the 20-20-20 eye rule, standing up and lunch), quiet hours and notification preferences;
  • your daily logs: when you logged water, coffee or tea, breaks, eye rests, stand-ups, focus blocks and lunch, and the reminders you answered “No”;
  • your achievements, streaks and focus timer;
  • your preferences, such as language, time format and theme;
  • technical flags, such as whether you finished the setup, whether we already asked you for a rating, your progress with rewarded videos and a local copy of your Premium status;
  • if you connect Health Connect: daily totals of steps, steps during work hours, minutes of sleep and wake-up time (see section 5).

Your wellness score, insights, achievements and the home-screen widget are calculated on your phone from this data. The widget shows your progress on your home screen, so anyone who can see your screen can see it.

Daily logs older than 400 days are deleted automatically. Everything else stays until you delete it (see section 11).

Notifications. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. DeskFlow doesn't use any push notification service and never sends a push token anywhere. A push messaging component (Firebase Cloud Messaging) comes bundled with a notifications library the app uses, but DeskFlow never activates it.

CSV report. If you choose to download your report, DeskFlow creates a CSV file on your phone with your daily totals for the last 30 days (including steps and minutes of sleep if Health Connect is connected) and opens Android's share sheet. The file only goes where you choose to save or send it.

4. Data processed by other companies

In short: Only a few partners process data, each for a clear reason: Google AdMob for ads, Google Play for payments, RevenueCat to validate purchases, and Android's backup service if you use it.

4.1 Google AdMob: ads in the free version

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, together with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), which asks for your consent where required.

  • Ad formats: banners; native ads, which match the app's design and are labeled “Ad”; interstitials, full-screen ads shown only at natural breaks such as after finishing an exercise or a focus block, and at most about once every 30 minutes; and rewarded videos, which you only watch if you choose to (see below).
  • What Google may collect: your advertising ID and app set ID; your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location; device and operating system information (such as model, Android version and language); your interactions with ads and app launches; and diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK. Google may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox ad APIs (Topics, Attribution Reporting and the advertising ID offered through Android's ad services), which you control in Android's settings.
  • Why: to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and detect and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Personalized ads: ads are personalized only where you consent or where the law allows it. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it's required, Google's consent form appears before ads can be personalized; if you don't consent, Google shows non-personalized or limited ads. Where Google's consent message applies, DeskFlow's Settings include an Ad privacy option to review or change your choice at any time.
  • Your controls on any Android phone: reset or delete your advertising ID and adjust Android's ad privacy settings (how). If you're signed in to Google, you can also manage ad personalization in My Ad Center.
  • Premium: with Premium, DeskFlow doesn't load or show ads. The ads library may still start when you open the app and check your consent status.
  • No health data: Health Connect data is never shared with Google AdMob or any ad network and is never used to choose ads.

Rewarded videos

Rewarded videos are always optional and only start when you tap. They unlock one CSV report download after 3 videos; the smartwatch connection, once, after 3 videos (Premium users skip this); and some Premium features, such as monthly insights and the full exercise library, for 24 hours after 1 video. A video counts only when watched to the end, and your progress is saved on your phone. Rewarded videos are ads served by Google AdMob as described above. Unlocking the watch connection doesn't link any health data to ads: it's just a flag saved on your phone.

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, Google's advertising technologies and the Google Privacy Policy.

4.2 Google Play Billing: payments

DeskFlow Premium is sold as monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and as a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never see or store your card or payment details.

Google shares with us limited order information needed for accounting, refunds and support (for example order number, product, date, price and country) and lets the app check whether your purchase is active. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4.3 RevenueCat: purchase validation

We use RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) to validate purchases and keep your Premium status in sync. When the app starts, it checks your Premium status with RevenueCat using an anonymous ID generated in the app. If you buy or restore Premium, RevenueCat also receives your purchase token and receipt from Google Play, the product, its price and currency, the store country and the transaction dates (purchase, renewal, expiry or refund).

The anonymous ID isn't linked to your name, email or card. Like any online service, RevenueCat's servers see your IP address when the app connects. RevenueCat acts as our service provider (processor) and processes this data only on our behalf. Access is limited to us, through RevenueCat's dashboard, and to RevenueCat and its own service providers. Learn more: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4.4 Android backup (Google)

DeskFlow allows Android Auto Backup. If backup by Google is turned on on your phone, Android may store a copy of DeskFlow's data (including your logs and, if you connected Health Connect, the daily totals of steps and sleep) in your Google account, and restore it when you reinstall DeskFlow or set up a new phone. On Android 9 and later, if your phone has a screen lock, app backups are end-to-end encrypted with your PIN, pattern or password. We can't access this backup.

You control it in your phone's settings (for example Settings → Google → Backup; the path varies by phone). Learn more: Back up or restore data on your Android device.

4.5 Ratings and sharing

  • Google Play In-App Review: after some achievements, DeskFlow may ask Google Play, once, to show its rating window. Google decides whether to show it. If you leave a review, Google handles it under its own terms; we only see what's public on Google Play.
  • Android share sheet: “Share DeskFlow” (a message with the Google Play link) and the CSV report use Android's share sheet. The content only goes to the app or person you choose.

DeskFlow doesn't include analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services, social sign-in or data brokers.

5. Health Connect

In short: Only if you allow it, DeskFlow reads your steps and sleep from Health Connect. Access is read-only, the data is processed on your phone, and it's never uploaded, shared, sold or used for ads. You can turn it off at any time.

DeskFlow is a general wellness app, not a medical device. It doesn't provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

DeskFlow's use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What DeskFlow reads

  • Steps (android.permission.health.READ_STEPS)
  • Sleep sessions (android.permission.health.READ_SLEEP)

Nothing else. DeskFlow doesn't ask for permission to write and never writes, changes or deletes anything in Health Connect.

When

Health Connect is optional. DeskFlow reads nothing until you open Settings → Watch & health, tap to connect and grant access on Health Connect's own permission screen. In the free version, you first unlock the watch connection once by watching 3 rewarded videos (Premium users don't need to). This doesn't give the ad network any health data.

How it's used

Only for features you can see in the app:

  • Steps: the steps you take during the work hours you set count toward your circulation (stand up and move) goal and your wellness score. Today's total appears in the app, and Insights shows your average.
  • Sleep: DeskFlow looks at your most recent sleep session (ending between 6 p.m. the day before and 2 p.m. today) to find your real wake-up time. This adjusts the suggested time for your coffee or tea and shows how long you slept. Insights shows your average.

Where it's processed and what's stored

DeskFlow reads the data on your phone when you open or return to the app (at most once every couple of minutes) or when you tap “Refresh now”. It saves only four daily values in your daily log on the phone: today's steps, steps during work hours, minutes of sleep and wake-up time. Individual Health Connect records aren't stored.

Limited Use, in plain words

  • No transfers, except to provide the features. We never send your Health Connect data to our servers (we don't have any) or to anyone else. It only leaves the app through actions you control: the CSV report you can export includes daily steps and minutes of sleep and goes only where you send it, and if Google backup is on, the daily totals are part of the encrypted app backup in your own Google account.
  • No ads. We never use it to serve ads, for ad targeting or personalized or interest-based advertising, or to build profiles.
  • No sale. We never sell or transfer it to third parties, including advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers.
  • No human reading. No person reads your health data. We have no access to it at all.
  • No other purposes. We never use it to determine creditworthiness, insurance eligibility or employment suitability, for lending, or for anything other than the features above. For example, it isn't used to train AI models.

Legal basis (GDPR and UK GDPR)

Health data is a special category of personal data (Art. 9 GDPR). Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies, the legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a), together with Art. 6(1)(a)), which you give on Health Connect's permission screen. You can withdraw it at any time; this doesn't affect the app's other features or the lawfulness of processing before you withdrew it.

Your control

  • Tap Stop using health data in Settings → Watch & health: DeskFlow stops reading Health Connect right away.
  • Revoke the permissions in Health Connect: in DeskFlow, tap Manage permissions in Health Connect, or open Health Connect and go to App permissions → DeskFlow (step by step).
  • Daily totals already saved stay in your logs on your phone until you clear DeskFlow's data or uninstall the app, or until they're deleted automatically after 400 days.
  • DeskFlow works fully without Health Connect.

The privacy link on Health Connect's permission screen opens DeskFlow's privacy screen, which you can also reach from Settings → About → Privacy policy and from the Watch & health screen.

If you live in a U.S. state with consumer health data laws (such as Washington, Nevada or Connecticut), see also our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

8. International transfers

In short: The data you keep in DeskFlow isn't transferred to us at all. Google and RevenueCat may process the data they receive in the United States and other countries, with recognized safeguards.

  • We're based in Colombia. Because we don't run servers, the data you keep in DeskFlow isn't transferred to us. If you email us, your message reaches us there.
  • Google (AdMob, Google Play and backup) and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and in other countries where they or their providers operate. These countries may have data protection laws different from yours.
  • Where required, these transfers rely on adequacy decisions; the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies; the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and their UK and Swiss equivalents; or other mechanisms recognized by your local law. See Google's data transfer frameworks and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

9. Retention

In short: On your phone, data stays until you delete it (daily logs for at most 400 days). Our partners keep their data under their own policies, and we keep purchase records only as long as needed.

  • On your phone: until you clear DeskFlow's data or uninstall the app. Daily logs older than 400 days, including the Health Connect daily totals, are deleted automatically.
  • Android backup: as long as your Google account keeps it under Google's backup settings. You can turn it off or delete it.
  • Google AdMob: according to Google's policies (how Google retains data).
  • Google Play: Google keeps purchase records according to its policies and legal obligations.
  • RevenueCat purchase record: while your Premium is active (for a lifetime purchase, while we offer the app), and afterwards only as long as needed for refunds, disputes, fraud prevention and our legal obligations, such as tax and accounting. Then we delete or anonymize it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Emails: as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up, then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

10. Security

In short: Your data stays in your phone's protected app storage, connections to our partners are encrypted, and with no servers of our own there's no DeskFlow database to breach. If a breach ever happened, we'd notify you as the law requires.

  • No servers: we don't operate servers, so there's no DeskFlow database of user logs or health data that could be breached.
  • Android app sandbox: app data is kept in DeskFlow's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. On phones with a screen lock, Android also encrypts the storage.
  • Encryption in transit: the Google and RevenueCat SDKs in the app connect to their services over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Our accounts: we limit access to our developer accounts (Google Play Console, AdMob and RevenueCat) and protect them with strong passwords and two-step verification.
  • Your part: use a screen lock, keep your phone updated and be careful about where you share CSV reports.

Breach response

We don't disclose health data without your authorization. If a security breach affecting your personal data ever occurred, we would notify the affected users and the competent authorities without undue delay where the law requires it (for example, the supervisory authority within 72 hours under the GDPR, when required). For identifiable health information, we would also notify the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) and, where required, the media, as set out in the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (16 CFR Part 318).
